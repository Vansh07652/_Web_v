# Content coverage report

Generated by `scripts/build-coverage-report.mjs` from `content-v2/` and the metadata sidecar in `content-v2/indexes/`. Do not edit by hand.

## What these numbers mean

Coverage here counts **what exists**, not what is correct. A topic is counted as having college notes, an ELI-10 explanation or references when its bundle says that section is present. A course scoring well has material in most sections and questions attached to it.

## What these numbers do not mean

- They are not a quality or accuracy score. Nothing is counted as good, current or clinically safe.
- **No content in this repository has a documented credentialed clinical review.** `Published` means the material is grounded in cited source text and complete enough to study, nothing more.
- `Citation review` counts questions the importers flagged for citation checking. It is a workflow flag on the question, not a judgement about the topic it maps to.
- Question counts include every question in a course's bank: exact-mapped, subject-wide overlay and broad-source-topic placements alike. The same question can be counted once per course it is placed in.
- `Held` topics are flagged for medical review and are deliberately withheld from study. `Draft` topics are incomplete. Neither is published.
